Frequently Asked Questions

Drag one element onto another on the forge screen. If the combination is valid, a new element will appear with a discovery animation. You start with four elements — Fire, Water, Air, and Earth — and build from there.
The Master Forger upgrade unlocks recipe hints for undiscovered elements. Open the grimoire, find any undiscovered element, and the hint will show you which elements to combine. Think logically — most recipes follow natural patterns. For example, Water + Earth = Mud, and Fire + Mud = Brick.
The grimoire is your personal encyclopedia of discoveries. It organizes all 208 elements into 16 categories — Nature, Mythical, Space, Pop Culture, and more. Tap any element to read its story, see how it was created, and discover what it can create next.
Elements have six rarity tiers: Primordial, Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, and Legendary. Rarer elements have more spectacular discovery effects — from gentle sparkles for Common to screen-shaking gold rain for Legendary. The rarity reflects how advanced the element is in the discovery tree.

Quick Tips

Think logically

Every recipe follows real-world logic. Water + Earth = Mud. Fire + Mud = Brick. Brick + Brick = Wall. Follow the chain and the discoveries will come naturally.

Use the grimoire

Tap any discovered element in the grimoire to see what it creates. This helps you find new combinations using elements you already have.

Try everything

There are 266 recipes hidden across 9 tiers. Don't be afraid to experiment — some of the most surprising discoveries come from unexpected combinations.
