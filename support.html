Frequently Asked Questions

Drag one element onto another on the forge screen. If the combination is valid, a new element will appear with a discovery animation. You start with four elements — Fire, Water, Air, and Earth — and build from there. Every recipe follows real-world logic, so think about what might happen when two things meet.
The Master Forger upgrade unlocks recipe hints. Open the grimoire, tap any undiscovered element, and you'll see which ingredients to combine. You can also tap the "?" on the forge screen for a hint. Think logically — most recipes follow natural patterns. For example, Water + Earth = Mud, and Fire + Mud = Brick.
The grimoire is your personal encyclopedia of discoveries. It organizes all 208 elements into 16 categories — Nature, Mythical, Space, Pop Culture, and more. Tap any element to read its story, see how it was created, and discover what it can create next.
Elements span six rarity levels: Primordial, Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, and Legendary. Rarer elements have more spectacular discovery effects — from gentle sparkles for Common to screen-shaking gold rain for Legendary. The rarity reflects how advanced the element is in the discovery tree and spans 13 tiers of complexity.

Quick Tips

Think logically

Every recipe follows real-world logic. Water + Earth = Mud. Fire + Mud = Brick. Brick + Brick = Wall. Follow the chain.

Use the grimoire

Tap any discovered element in the grimoire to see what it creates. This helps you find new combinations using elements you already have.

Experiment freely

There are 266 recipes hidden across 13 tiers. Some of the most surprising discoveries come from unexpected combinations.
