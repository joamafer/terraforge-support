About Terraforge

Start with Fire, Water, Air, and Earth. Combine elements to discover Dragons, Galaxies, and the Universe itself.

Terraforge is an element-combining puzzle game set in 2500 AD, where humanity's last alchemist must rebuild civilization on a barren planet. Players begin with four primordial elements and forge their way through 208 elements across 266 recipes and 13 tiers of discovery.

Every recipe follows real-world logic. Every discovery is accompanied by rarity-scaled particle effects, haptic feedback, and synthesized sound — all generated at runtime. Six rarity tiers reward curiosity with increasingly spectacular celebrations, from a gentle sparkle for Common elements to screen-shaking gold rain for Legendary ones.

Built entirely in SwiftUI with zero third-party dependencies. The generative ambient soundtrack, discovery chimes, and all sound effects are synthesized at runtime via AVAudioEngine. CoreHaptics patterns scale from a subtle tap to a legendary rumble. iCloud syncs progress across devices. Game Center ranks discoveries globally.

Fully localized in English, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese — including all 208 element names, descriptions, and lore. VoiceOver supported. Reduce Motion honored. Zero data collected.
