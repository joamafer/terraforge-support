Privacy Policy

Zero data collection. By design.

Last updated: March 12, 2026

Terraforge does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your privacy is fully protected.

Overview

Terraforge is an element combination game designed with privacy as a core principle. We believe you should be able to enjoy our game without any concerns about your personal information.

Data We Don't Collect

To be completely clear, Terraforge does not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavior tracking
  • Device identifiers for advertising
  • Game progress or play patterns
  • Any data that could identify you personally

Local Storage

Your game progress — discovered elements, milestones, and preferences — is stored locally on your device using the Keychain. This data is not transmitted to any server or third party. If you have iCloud Keychain enabled on your device, iOS may sync Keychain items across your devices as part of Apple's standard behavior.

iCloud Sync

If you have iCloud enabled, Terraforge will sync your game progress across your devices using Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage. This data is managed entirely by Apple and is not accessible to us. You can disable iCloud sync in your device settings.

In-App Purchases

Terraforge offers an optional one-time purchase (Master Forger). This transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not process, store, or have access to any payment information.

Game Center

Terraforge supports Game Center for leaderboards. Game Center is managed entirely by Apple. Your Game Center alias and discovery count may be visible to other players on the leaderboard. We do not access or store your Game Center profile information.

No Account Required

Terraforge does not require you to create an account or sign in. All features are available immediately after download with no registration needed.

Offline Functionality

Terraforge works completely offline. No internet connection is required to play the game. Terraforge itself does not transmit any data to external servers. When online, only Apple's own services (iCloud, Game Center, and the App Store) may communicate as part of their standard functionality.

No Third-Party Services

We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no SDKs in Terraforge that collect or transmit your data.

Children's Privacy

Terraforge does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The game is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our privacy practices, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above. Given our commitment to not collecting data, we do not anticipate significant changes.
